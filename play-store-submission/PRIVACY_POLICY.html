Privacy Policy — KasbPro PDF Editor

Effective Date: October 21, 2026
Developer: kasb LLC
Application ID: com.kasbllc.kasbpropdf

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

KasbPro PDF Editor is engineered as a 100% free, ad-free, offline-first utility. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, documents, telemetry, or analytics on any kasb LLC servers.

2. On-Device Document Processing

All PDF viewing, editing, text replacement, signatures, stamps, Word/Excel table conversions, and OCR recognition happen strictly locally on your device using on-device storage (IndexedDB).

3. Optional User-Initiated Cloud Sync

If you voluntarily connect your own third-party cloud storage account (such as Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox, or your own WebDAV server), your files are transmitted directly between your device and your chosen storage provider under your own credentials.

4. Zero Advertisements & Zero Tracking

KasbPro PDF Editor contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no paywalls.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact kasb LLC at support@kasb.com.
