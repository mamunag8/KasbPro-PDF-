# KasbPro-PDF-
## KasbPro PDF Editor & Reader

**100% Free • Zero Ads • Zero Paywalls • 100% Offline-First PDF Viewer, Book Reader, Editor, Local Signature/Seal Studio, Multi-Format Table-Preserving Converter, OCR & Cloud Sync Suite**  
Built by **kasb LLC** for **Web (PWA), Android, and iOS**.

---

## Core Capabilities

1. **4 Structured Viewing & Reading Modes**:
   - **Common Mode**: Full PDF editing workspace with interactive canvas, clickable in-place PDF text replacement, highlights, sticky notes, inline editable tables, signatures, official seals, business stamps, freehand ink, shapes, and redaction.
   - **Book Mode (`Book Content Mode`)**: 2-Page Book Spread (with realistic spine shadow) or 1-Page Focused View, auto-extracted hierarchical Table of Contents (TOC) drawer, page bookmarks, and reading progress bar.
   - **Reading Mode (`Comfort Reflow Mode`)**: Reflows headings, paragraphs, and **structured tables** into an eye-comfort layout with **Day Paper**, **Warm Sepia**, **Eye-Care Sage**, and **Night Dark** themes, typography controls, and **Offline Read-Aloud (TTS)**.
   - **One-Click Full Screen**: Distraction-free immersive mode (`F11`).

2. **100% Local Signature, Official Seal & Stamp Vault**:
   - **Draw Handwritten Signatures**: Pressure-smooth signature pad with multi-color ink.
   - **Import Local Sign & Seal Images**: Import `.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`, or `.svg` photos of physical signatures or company stamps with real-time **automatic white-background removal** (`Remove White Background (Auto Transparent)`) and adjustable threshold slider.
   - **Official Circular Seal Builder**: Generate double-ring circular company seals with organization name, center status/title, registration/date, and ink color.
   - **Local Vault & Portable Packs**: Save unlimited signs and seals in local `IndexedDB`, download transparent `.png` files, or export/import `.json` packs.

3. **Multi-Format Viewer & Table-Preserving PDF Converter**:
   - Open and convert `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.xls`, `.csv`, `.tsv`, `.md`, `.html`, `.txt`, and images (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`, `.svg`).
   - Preserves multi-column tables, borders, cell alignments, and repeating headers across PDF page breaks.
   - Extract any table from a PDF page directly to **Excel (`.xlsx`)** or **CSV (`.csv`)**.

4. **Offline OCR & Table Extractor**:
   - Scan full pages or drag a marquee box over any region (`OCR & Tables` tool) with Auto-Contrast and Binarization filters.

5. **Offline-First Vault + Multi-Cloud Storage Sync**:
   - Automatic local persistence in `IndexedDB` (`KasbProVaultDB`) with an **Offline Sync Queue**.
   - Sync with **Google Drive**, **Microsoft OneDrive**, **Dropbox**, **Nextcloud / WebDAV**, or **Local Synced Cloud Folders**.

6. **12+ Global Languages (LTR & RTL)**:
   - English, Arabic (`RTL`), Persian (`RTL`), Urdu (`RTL`), Bengali, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Russian, and Turkish.
